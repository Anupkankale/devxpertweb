Why the lab exists

One real problem at a time.

Five years studying Computer Science, a bachelor's then a master's, followed by four years of corporate job experience and contributing to WordPress core and the wider open-source community. Somewhere in there, a pattern became hard to ignore.

The same real-world problems keep coming back, and most tools answer them by piling on complexity instead of removing it. DevXpert Labs is the response: pick one problem, understand it properly, and build the smallest thing that genuinely solves it.

About the lab

Run by Anup. Powered by curiosity.

DevXpert Labs is the experimental workshop of Anup Kankale, a WordPress & PHP developer from Mumbai. It's the place where client deadlines and personal what-ifs get worked out with the same care.

The lab exists for one reason: to keep asking how things actually work, then build the answer. The useful experiments become plugins and tools; the rest become lessons.
