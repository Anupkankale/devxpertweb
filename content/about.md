DevXpert Labs is the experimental workshop of Anup Kankale, a WordPress & PHP developer from Mumbai. It's the place where client deadlines and personal what-ifs get worked out with the same care.

The lab exists for one reason: to keep asking how things actually work, then build the answer. The useful experiments become plugins and tools; the rest become lessons. Either way, something ships, usually alongside an uncountable number of cups of chai. ☕
