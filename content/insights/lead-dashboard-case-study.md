## The problem

A travel agency was drowning in form submissions from its website. Enquiries came in through Forminator and Contact Form 7, but there was no way to see which ones had been answered, who was handling them, or which had turned into bookings. A CRM would have solved it, but the agency was priced out.

## The approach

The enquiry data was already inside WordPress, so the lab didn't move it anywhere. Instead it built a plugin that treats each form entry as a lead, right in the WordPress admin:

- **Lead statuses** to track each enquiry from new to closed
- **Feedback notes** so the whole team shares the context
- **CSV export** for reporting or moving data elsewhere

## The result

The agency got lead tracking without a new tool, login or subscription. The plugin was then released for free on the official WordPress.org directory under the GPL, where it's rated 5 stars and available to any WordPress site with the same problem.
