Before DevXpert Labs ships anything of its own, it gives back. That's not a slogan. It's the order the lab actually works in.

## Credited on WordPress 7.0 "Armstrong"

WordPress 7.0 "Armstrong" shipped with 875-plus credited contributors, and I'm one of them. It's a core release used by millions of sites: code that helps everyone and is owned by no one.

[See the release credits](https://wordpress.org/news/2026/05/armstrong/)

## Beyond WordPress

The same habit extends past WordPress. I've also contributed to NudgeBee, pitching in on the [v1.3.0 release](https://github.com/nudgebee/nudgebee/releases/tag/v1.3.0) of an open-source tool for the wider developer community.

## Why do it for free?

Every tool I build my work on (WordPress, PHP, the libraries under them) was given to me the same way: freely, by people I'll never meet. Contributing back, **without expecting anything in return**, is how the lab pays that forward.

It also makes the client work better. Reading and contributing to core means understanding how WordPress actually works, not just how to configure it.
